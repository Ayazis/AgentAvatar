Rosie | Blender metal textures

Base color PNGs are sRGB and contain no directional lighting, ambient occlusion
or specular highlights. Use them with a metallic shader. Face PNGs are 2048 square,
orthographic and centered. The nominal outer diameter is 90% of the image width.
Their transparent openings show where to model bores and spoke cutouts.
The large gear has 40 teeth and seven spokes. These are new reference proportions,
not calibrated measurements of the earlier tilted art.

Height PNGs are 16-bit grayscale, aligned to the face textures. Use Non-Color,
connect to Bump Height, and adjust Distance for your model scale. Mid gray is the
plate surface, lighter features are raised, and darker features are recessed.
The black pixels outside the silhouette and in through-holes are outside the mesh.

Outer-edge strips are 4096 x 256. U travels once around the full perimeter, including
tooth flanks and roots for the gears, and V spans the modeled thickness. The grain
wraps in U. Stretch V to your mesh thickness; the image does not specify thickness.

The bronze ring has matching rivets on both faces. The bearing rear has an unlit
retaining ring in place of the amber front insert. The steel ring, large gear and
pinion can reuse the front artwork on the rear.
